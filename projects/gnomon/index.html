02 / BEHIND THE INTERFACE

A shared view
of the working day.

Gnomon connects the things a team needs throughout the day: who’s here, how time is recorded, when breaks begin and who’s taking time off.

This portfolio uses fictional people and local actions. The main app uses individual accounts, shared records and manager permissions. The demo role switch is only for exploring the interface.

01

Start the day in one place

Clock in and out, see the running timer and review your own attendance history. In this demo, actions stay in the current page and reset on reload.

02

Keep availability visible

See who’s working, on a break or on leave. Announce a break one hour ahead, with an option to cancel before it starts. Break time is excluded from worked hours.

03

Plan time away together

Request time off with Gnomon’s branded calendar. Managers can review pending requests and approve or decline them in the shared calendar.

04

Turn records into a useful view

Review team hours over a chosen period and export a fictional CSV. Switch between employee and manager demos using the control at the bottom of the sidebar.
