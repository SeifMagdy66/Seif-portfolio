THE ORIGINAL PROBLEM

The process was taking
the team’s time.

Every week, people searched for companies, checked whether their websites were active, extracted emails, found people by job title, checked roles on LinkedIn and copied the results into a sheet.

The work crossed several tools, but the connections between them depended on people repeating the same steps.

HOW THE WORKFLOW WAS BUILT

Understand it. Connect it. Make it repeatable.

The operation was mapped, its costly manual steps identified, and the tools connected through their APIs. Targeting, website research, contact enrichment, verification and structured output became parts of one workflow.

The aim was to give the team more capacity and more time for work that needed their judgment. The automation became part of Pollion’s lead-generation operations.
