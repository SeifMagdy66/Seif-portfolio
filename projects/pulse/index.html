04 / WHAT MAKES A DELIVERY SUCCESSFUL?

Read the evidence.
Understand the result.

Pulse assesses delivery against the project’s commitments. The findings explain what went well, where points were lost and what needs review.

ACTIONABLE REQUESTS FINDINGS REVIEW

A response is part of the delivery.

Pulse looks at actionable requests, acknowledgments, answers and important changes. The review explains whether the communication met the instructions, using the case’s evidence.

Was the request acknowledged? Was it answered properly? Were changes handled clearly?

Evidence gaps stay visible. Required-source failures lead to Technical Hold; material contradictions stay Pending Review. People use the findings alongside client feedback and their own judgment.
